Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Custom Minifigs Collector helps you manage a personal minifigure catalogue using a Google Spreadsheet and Google Drive folders that remain in your Google account.

Information we process

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address and basic profile information needed to authenticate you.
  • With your permission, the application accesses Google Sheets and Google Drive to find, create and update your collection spreadsheet and its associated photo folders.
  • Application preferences and collection configuration, including the identifier of your selected spreadsheet, may be stored in Firebase so they can be restored on your devices.
  • If you enable push reminders, OneSignal processes a browser push subscription and notification-delivery information for that device. A telephone number is not required.
  • Some preferences are stored locally in your browser.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide authentication, catalogue management, spreadsheet access, photo management, backup and synchronisation features. We do not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising.

Google API data

The application requests access only when needed to work with your collection spreadsheet and related Drive files. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Your collection spreadsheet and photos are stored in your Google account. Configuration data is processed by Google Firebase. When push reminders are enabled, notification subscription and delivery data is processed by OneSignal. Information is shared with service providers only as necessary to operate these features or when required by law.

Your choices

You can revoke the application’s Google access from your Google Account permissions. You can also remove the spreadsheet and folders from Google Drive and clear locally stored browser data. Revoking access prevents features that depend on Google Sheets or Drive from working.

Security and retention

We use Google authentication and reasonable technical measures to protect application data. Configuration is retained while needed to provide synchronisation, unless you request deletion or remove it through the applicable service.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact contact@cmcollector.com.
